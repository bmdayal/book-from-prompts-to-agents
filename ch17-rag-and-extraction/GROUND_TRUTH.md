# Questions and answers, written before any run

Answerable (the key facts must appear):
1. How many days do I have to return an item?  -> 30 days (from delivery)
2. How much do I have to spend to get free standard shipping?  -> $75 (or more)
3. How long is the warranty on a tent?  -> 2 years
4. My tent zipper broke after 3 months. Can I get my money back?  -> The 30-day refund window has passed, but the 2-year warranty covers broken zippers: repair or replacement, not a refund. (Needs BOTH docs 01 and 03.)
5. How many points do I earn per dollar, and what are 500 points worth?  -> 1 point per $1; 500 points = a $5 voucher

Not in the documents (the correct behavior is to say so, not to invent):
6. Do you offer gift wrapping?
7. What is the CEO's name?
