Our full help center:
- Returns. You can return unused items within 30 days of delivery for a full refund to your original payment method. Items must be in their original packaging. Return shipping is free for orders in the US.
- Shipping. Standard shipping takes 3 to 5 business days and is free on orders of $75 or more. Orders under $75 pay a flat $6.95. Express shipping takes 1 to 2 business days and costs $14.95. We ship to the US and Canada.
- Warranty. Tents and sleeping bags have a 2-year warranty against defects in materials and workmanship, such as broken zippers and seam failures. If a covered item fails, we repair it or send a replacement. The warranty does not cover damage from misuse or normal wear.
- Trail Points. Members earn 1 point for every $1 spent. Every 500 points can be exchanged for a $5 voucher. Points expire after 24 months without a purchase.
- Stores. Northwind has two retail stores, in Portland, Oregon and Denver, Colorado. Both are open 10:00 to 18:00 Monday to Saturday and closed on Sundays.
- Repair service. Out-of-warranty repairs on tents and packs cost a flat $25 plus return shipping. Typical turnaround is 10 business days. Start a repair request from your account page.
- Changing an order. You can change or cancel an order within 2 hours of placing it. After that the order moves to the warehouse and can no longer be changed. Contact support with your order number.
- Payment methods. We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, PayPal and Northwind gift cards. We do not accept checks or cash on delivery.
- Gift cards. Northwind gift cards come in amounts from $25 to $200 and never expire. They can be used online and in stores but cannot be exchanged for cash.
- Privacy. We never sell your personal information. We keep order records for 7 years for tax purposes. You can ask us to delete your account data by emailing the privacy team.
- Sizing. Our jackets run true to size. If you are between sizes, we suggest sizing up for layering. Size charts are on each product page.
- Support hours. Customer support is available Monday to Friday, 8:00 to 20:00 Pacific Time, by chat and by phone. Email replies usually arrive within one business day.
