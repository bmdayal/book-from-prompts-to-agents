# Find a Way to Automate a Chore

## Use it when

you do the same task every week and suspect there's a better way.

## The prompt

```text
I do this repetitive task: [DESCRIBE THE TASK].
Suggest three ways to reduce the effort, from simplest to
most advanced. Name the kind of tool, not just brands. For
each, say what could go wrong and what data would leave my
computer.
Do not give time estimates. Ask me how often I do the task
and how long it takes, and then estimate.
```

Fill every [BRACKET] before you run it.

## Why it works

a range from simple to advanced lets you start small, and the "what could go wrong" and "what data leaves" lines build caution into the answer.

## Watch out

the original version asked the AI to "estimate time saved per week" without knowing anything about you. Any number it gave would be invented. Now it has to ask first.
