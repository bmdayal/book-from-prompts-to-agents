# Learn Something New

## Use it when

you want to understand a topic quickly without being talked down to.

## The prompt

```text
Teach me [TOPIC]. I already know [WHAT YOU KNOW], but I am
new to this topic.
Use three short examples, one analogy, and two practice
questions. Put the answers after the questions so I can
try first.
If part of the topic is debated or changes quickly, tell
me which part.
```

Fill every [BRACKET] before you run it.

## Why it works

"I already know X" sets the level, the examples and analogy shape the format, and the last line makes the AI flag shaky ground instead of sounding sure about everything.

## Make it yours

be honest about what you know. "I know spreadsheets but not databases" gets a better lesson than "I'm a beginner".
